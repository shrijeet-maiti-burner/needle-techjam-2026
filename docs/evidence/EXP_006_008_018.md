# EXP-006/008/018 state, lexical, and robustness evidence

Status: current integrated candidate selected; robustness gate still open.

## Intent override

The diagnostic replay on all 30 override sessions predicted 12 hits for full
reset, 19 for preserving only the stable sentence-bounded shopping subject, and
27 for retaining all history. Clean official-evaluator runs on commit `87cac3f8`
confirmed the ordering:

| Policy | TechnicalScore | HR@10 | MRR | Override HR | Override MRR | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| full reset | 0.733757 | 0.835 | 0.563522 | 0.400000 | 0.248929 | control |
| preserve subject | 0.760863 | 0.870 | 0.578542 | 0.633333 | 0.349061 | accepted |
| no reset | 0.805669 | 0.910 | 0.640230 | 0.900000 | 0.760317 | rejected as unfaithful |

No-reset is an upper-bound diagnostic, not a shippable policy: it ranks with a
preference the user explicitly revoked. Subject preservation keeps only an
explicit sentence-bounded shopping subject when the correction is scoped to an
earlier preference. Broader intent changes still perform a full reset.

Combining subject preservation with popularity strength 0.20 produced the
current unperturbed result: TechnicalScore 0.771833, HR@10 0.890, MRR 0.566442,
MTTC 3.155, and override HR 0.733333.

## Aryaman lexical controls

PR #3 supplied stdlib-only deterministic normalization, bounded additive apparel
expansion, and an unwired fuzzy helper. The reviewed branch passed 65 tests and
was merged as `bc15a0a`. Only normalization and expansion were wired behind
controls; fuzzy matching remained unwired because no bounded vocabulary and
end-to-end gate justified it.

| Sparse query rewrite | TechnicalScore | HR@10 | MRR | Override HR | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| none | 0.771833 | 0.890 | 0.566442 | 0.733333 | retain |
| normalize | 0.759733 | 0.875 | 0.557444 | 0.633333 | reject |
| normalize + expand | 0.760971 | 0.875 | 0.562236 | 0.633333 | reject |

Both rewrites changed BM25 ranks enough to lose public targets. They remain
available for future typo/synonym slices but are disabled in the current preset.
No external model, model license, network call, or non-stdlib dependency was
introduced.

## Wording perturbations

The deterministic diagnostic wrapper changes each simulator message before the
agent sees it. The final valid runs used commit `cc78875f`; the unperturbed
control reproduced exactly.

| Input condition | TechnicalScore | HR@10 | MRR | Override HR | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| released wording | 0.771833 | 0.890 | 0.566442 | 0.733333 | control |
| case/spacing/Unicode plus punctuation removal | 0.709905 | 0.830 | 0.492018 | 0.366667 | robustness failure |
| deterministic template paraphrase | 0.732517 | 0.850 | 0.526058 | 0.600000 | robustness failure |

Two earlier surface runs are invalidated. Whitespace expansion prevented the
old override regex from firing, silently turning them into no-reset behavior.
Their apparently high scores are excluded. A regression test now proves that
surface noise cannot carry the revoked preference across the override.

The expanded explicit-marker family improved the paraphrase result from a
diagnostic 0.718980 to 0.732517 while leaving the released control byte-for-
metric unchanged. Further phrase patches were stopped to avoid tuning against
our own perturbation set.

Aryaman's PR #4 perturbation library passed its isolated 94-test suite but is
not merged yet: it contains no official-loop session driver or slice report, and
some claimed meaning-preserving transforms still need scope-sensitive tests.
