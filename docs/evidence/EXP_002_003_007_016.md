## Signature scan

The catalog-bound scan covered all 50,000 products and generated 525,319
product-signature pairs across 222,886 buckets. Of those buckets, 89.3735% were
singletons; bucket size had median 1, p90 2, p99 14, and maximum 25,451.

On the 200 public targets, exact target-derived constraints retained the target
in all 200 cases at each one-through-four-constraint prefix. Median bucket size
for those prefixes was 7,027, 41, 2, and 1. This is evaluator-coupled diagnostic
evidence, not an official score or a private-set estimate.

The prebuilt SQLite asset is catalog-bound by schema version, catalog hash, and
product count. Its SHA-256 is
`5227de31b530bbdb46adcea59186186bdde9abfc774df571bf894275354494fd` and

## Bounded popularity prior

All arms used signature limit 100 over the same candidate pool. The signal is
`log1p(rating_number)` normalized to `[0, 1]`, added as a bounded reranking term;
it never filters a product.

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.00 | 0.718007 | 0.825 | 0.537024 | 3.780 |
| 0.02 | 0.723345 | 0.830 | 0.543149 | 3.730 |
| 0.05 | 0.730050 | 0.835 | 0.553500 | 3.675 |
| 0.10 | 0.733757 | 0.835 | 0.563522 | 3.640 |
| 0.20 | 0.734045 | 0.840 | 0.547484 | 3.510 |

Before inspecting per-target outcomes, the low-volume slice was fixed as the
lowest 50 public targets by `rating_number`, with `sample_id` breaking ties.
The slice spans rating counts 1 through 863; the next target has 986. The
no-prior control hit 42/50. Strength 0.02 retained 42/50; 0.05, 0.10, and 0.20
hit 43/50. No prior arm lost a control hit on this slice.

With subject-preserving override handling, strength 0.20 beat 0.10 by four hits
without losing any hit overall or in the low-volume slice. It therefore became
the current candidate. The preregistered hard-filter arm has not been run; it is
a missing deliberately failing diagnostic, not a candidate implementation.

## Resources

Adjacent clean runs on commit `708c0fb6` measured the sparse control at 9.325 s
startup, 101.851 s evaluation, and 353.7 MiB peak process memory. The prebuilt
signature arm measured 9.247 s startup, 85.251 s evaluation, and 356.1 MiB peak
process memory. Timing varies materially with machine load, so only adjacent
runs are compared.

An earlier in-process signature implementation took 23.5 s startup and peaked
at 174.6 MiB traced Python memory. It was a dirty diagnostic, was rejected, and
does not support the selected implementation.

## Decision

Retain signature-first promotion with bucket limit 100, the official field
weights, slate size 10, no seen-item exclusion, and popularity strength 0.20.
The pure sparse path remains the packaging and robustness rollback. This choice
is provisional because EXP-018 still shows material wording sensitivity and the
30.6 MiB asset has not passed a final clean-bundle rehearsal.
